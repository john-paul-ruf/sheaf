Implementation contract · DEC-71
  • One producer. First accepted authored change triggers MOD-001 after durable local acknowledgement. Record create/edit/delete/restore, schema commands, saved/pinned charts and theme all use the same producer. Multi-event commands count once. Rejected/no-op commands, drafts, search, recalculation and last-opened do not trigger. Imports/reconciliation count toward pending backup facts but do not trigger an authored-change prompt.
  • Approved schedule. After successive dismissals, no sooner than 10 minutes, then 1 hour, then 24 hours, then daily while scratch. Dismissal persists as encrypted operational state, survives restart and is not an authored change. No reload, route switch or extra edit resets the interval or creates duplicate dialogs. Use the policy clock, including rollback handling; never infer time from commit counts.
  • Never block work. The local change is already accepted. “Keep working”, Escape and close dismiss and restore the exact caller; do not undo or delay the save. Badge, last confirmed time and authoritative pending count remain on tile and app. “Back up now” and “Choose a durable home” lead to the existing chooser. Bundle save links to its detailed mock after the existing vault setup.
  • Escalation and accuracy. First reminder explains scratch; later reminders name the current exact device-only count and repeat the loss consequence without implying edits are forbidden. Display counts from the current app/home. Ignore stale dismissal responses from another trigger/app/home. Assigning a home suppresses scratch reminders and removes the scratch badge, but never fabricates a backup receipt or zero pending count.
  • Keyboard and focus. Native dialog contains focus; heading receives initial focus. Tab/Shift+Tab cycle actions; Enter activates the focused action. Escape equals “Keep working”; backdrop does nothing. Closing restores launcher; if removed in production, focus the enclosing app heading. Announce local success before the reminder, with no duplicate announcements. Buttons stay at least 44px; warning includes text as well as color.
  • Layout. 320–599px bottom sheet with 16px gutters, stacked actions and safe-area padding; scroll the sheet at short heights. 600–899px centered 480px single-column dialog, 24px gutters. At 900px use 88px rail and split workspace; at 1200px use 248px labelled rail. The warning is visible at every width and after dismissal.
